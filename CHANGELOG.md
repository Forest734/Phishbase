## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-27

### Added

- Phishbase: Deadbase, the Grateful Dead setlist browser, remade for Phish,
  with the same Years, Songs, show, search and Stats pages, sortable lists,
  overview charts and Start over button.
- Every known Phish show, 1983–2026, with setlists, from the
  [phish.in](https://phish.in) API (open source, no key needed). Encores,
  third sets and soundchecks are labelled as phish.in records them.
- Show notes on each show page: the notes on each song (Phish.net Jam Charts
  write-ups, guests, teases, debuts, bustouts, alternate lyrics, signals,
  banter), notes on the show itself (lore, costumes, weather), and the taper
  notes that came with the recording. Song lengths, and a link to listen on
  phish.in.
- Concert photos from Wikimedia Commons on the shows they were taken at, each
  credited to its photographer with its licence, and phish.in's generated
  cover art for every show with a recording.
- A Tours page, and tour and tag filters: every show on a tour, or with a tag
  such as Jam Charts, Guest or Debut. A song's page links to its Jam Charts
  versions, and ★ marks Jam Charts picks in year-page setlists.
- Stats for Phish's eras (1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 4.0), with Jam Charts leaders and
  show features in place of Deadbase's segue chart, and a longest-song record.
- A donut logo beside each page title and as the favicon, traced with potrace
  from the project artwork.
